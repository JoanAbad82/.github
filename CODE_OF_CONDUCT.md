# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributions and discussions should be respectful, technically specific, and focused on evidence, reproducibility, and the work itself.

- Critique ideas, code, documentation, and evidence rather than people.
- State uncertainty and limitations when they materially affect a claim.
- Do not submit secrets, credentials, private data, harassment, spam, or intentionally deceptive material.
- Respect repository-local security, research, and production boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and repeated bad-faith interaction are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, close, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. Security-sensitive reports should follow SECURITY.md rather than public discussion.
